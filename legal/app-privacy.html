Privacy Policy — Bioconnect for iPhone

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the Bioconnect app for iPhone. The website version has its own privacy policy.

Who we are

Bioconnect is made by 生方 天, an individual in Japan. Contact: ubu_ten0315@icloud.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics, and it does not send anything about you or your games to us. The only other company that receives information from the app is Google, for ads (see below).

Your scores, settings and game progress are saved only on your iPhone. They stay there until you delete the app.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional ad you can watch to revive, and a short ad when you start a new game after a game over. Ads are never shown to players who own No Ads.

We ask Google for non-personalized ads only, and the app never asks to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Non-personalized ads are chosen from things like the app itself and your general location (such as country), not from your interests or history. To show and measure ads, limit how often an ad is shown and prevent fraud, Google’s ad software receives information such as your IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Where the law requires consent (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for it before any ad is shown, and you can change your choice later with “Ad privacy choices” in the store.

Purchases

Crystals and No Ads are bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app keeps only your Crystal balance, whether you own No Ads, and the purchase numbers Apple gives it, on your iPhone, so that a purchase is not added twice.

Links that leave the app

If you choose to share a score, the app opens X (formerly Twitter) with a post you can edit. Anything you post there is handled under X’s own privacy policy. Opening an email link uses your own mail app.

Children

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting more information, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
