Privacy Policy — Bioconnect for iPhone

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy covers the Bioconnect app for iPhone. The website version has its own privacy policy.

Who we are

Bioconnect is made by 生方 天, an individual in Japan. Contact: ubu_ten0315@icloud.com.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send anything about you or your games to us or to anyone else.

Your scores, settings and game progress are saved only on your iPhone. They stay there until you delete the app.

Links that leave the app

If you choose to share a score, the app opens X (formerly Twitter) with a post you can edit. Anything you post there is handled under X’s own privacy policy. Opening an email link uses your own mail app.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts collecting information, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
