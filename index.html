How to play

  1. 1. Plant without waiting

    Tap an empty square. Your first tile is placed and immediately replaced.

  2. 2. One path to the elephant

    Match two: sprout → grass → rabbit → fox → deer → zebra → buffalo → wolf → bear → lion → tiger → elephant. The progression is a discovery path; diets are shown below.

  3. 3. Plan your chain

    Growth stays on an existing matching tile. Ties use topmost, then leftmost. Merge and Chain labels preview your move. Later chain steps clear all blockers touching the merging group.

  4. 4. Keep the food chain fed

    Herbivores eat grass or sprouts. Foxes eat rabbits or plants. Wolves hunt rabbits, foxes, deer and zebras. Bears prefer grass, then rabbits or deer. Lions and tigers hunt deer, zebras and buffalo. Animals eat only when hungry. Feeding creates space and scores points.

  5. 5. The elephant eats only what you raised

    An elephant ignores plants. It takes animals you grew by merging — marked with a purple dot from the tiger onward. It also gets hungry about two and a half times faster than anything else, and pays four times a normal meal. Reach it with raised animals in reserve, or it leaves bones.

  6. 6. Start small, grow big

    Your hand always contains sprouts and grass in the same mix. Every animal, from rabbit to elephant, is earned by merging. Discoveries never add bigger tiles to your hand.

  7. 7. Meet the next challenge

    Over time, stones arrive more often and plants gradually fade sooner. Winter keeps getting harder, up to Winter 5. Growth and meal points rise from ×1 to ×8 before the score scale is applied. Watch the countdown, and merge beside blockers to clear them. A full board ends the run; waiting alone earns no points.

  8. 8. A complete meadow

    Discover all ten animals to welcome the elephant, then keep playing for a higher score. Chains, simultaneous meals and seasons multiply your points.

  9. 9. Choose your pace and sound

    Relaxed doubles the time between world updates. The guide and hidden tabs pause the world. Sound and Music start off; turn them on independently.
