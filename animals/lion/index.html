The Lion

The lion is the king of the beasts, and it would like you to know that it has the crown to prove it. That is about all the proof there is. On a normal day the lion lies flat on the grass, yawns until its eyes water, and does not get up for anybody. There is no dignity in it at all. And yet the lion really is one of the strongest animals on the meadow. It just only shows it once a day, when it gets hungry.

Place on the food chain

Eats
Foxes, deer, zebras, buffalo, wolves and bears. Never plants. The animal right in front of its face goes first.
Eaten by
Tigers. The elephant will also eat any lion, since every lion is one you raised.
Appetite
Very patient. It can lie there for a long time. When it does get hungry, it means it.

The laziest king

Most of the time, the lion does nothing. It yawns. It yawns again. The crown stays on, mostly out of habit. Animals walk right past it and it does not even look. If you put a lion on the board and wonder whether it is doing anything, the answer is no.

Serious only when hungry

Then it gets hungry, and for a moment the lion remembers who it is. The yawn closes, the face goes hard, and it eats one animal touching it. It doesn't touch the grass. A king eats meat.

It doesn't shop around, either. The lion faces right, and the animal on its right, straight in front of its face, is the one it eats first. That counts even if it is a wolf or a bear you spent ages building, and even if a cheap fox from your hand is waiting on its other side. Only when nothing is in front of it does the lion look around, and then it takes the cheapest animal beside it, a dealt one before one you raised.

Mind what you put in front of it

This is the one thing to remember about the lion. Its right side is its plate. Park your best animal there and, the next time the lion gets hungry, it is gone. Park something you can spare there instead, and you choose its dinner for it.

The plan: two lions make a tiger

  • Build it from bears. Two bears side by side make a lion. Your hand never deals one, so every lion is built.
  • Keep your best animals off its right side. A wolf or a bear in front of a hungry lion is a wasted wolf or bear.
  • Serve dinner on purpose. A dealt fox or deer on its right is a meal you picked. It keeps the lion away from everything else.
  • Watch the hunger bar, not the yawn. The lion looks the same lazy king right up until it gets hungry. The red bar is the only warning you get.
  • Pair it up for a tiger. Two lions merge into a tiger. Build the second one right beside the first, and not in front of it.

Real lions

Real lions really are lazy, at least by the clock. They often sleep or rest for 16 to 20 hours a day, saving their energy in the heat. When they do hunt, it is short and serious: a burst of speed and a lot of strength over a very short distance. Lions are also the only big cats that live in groups, called prides. In a pride, it is mostly the lionesses who do the hunting, while the males with the big manes guard the territory. So the king is not just lazy. In real life, he also lets someone else catch dinner.
